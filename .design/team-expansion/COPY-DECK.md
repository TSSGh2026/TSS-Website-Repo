## Page-level copy

**Movement label:** "The Specialists" (Fatema's word, 18 Sep). The 30 Sep doc
calls the section "Partners". The copy review argues for neither: Specialists
sets them against the founders as a tier, Partners claims a commercial
relationship. Its pick is "The Collective", but the page's hero eyebrow already
says that, so it would need a change there too.

**Standfirst (B, unchanged):** The collective that holds your story together.
One team on the brief, from the thinking to the making.

**Hero:** "Senior strategists, and the specialists they work with." →
"Strategists, writers, designers and growth marketers, working as one team."
The old line ranked the second movement under the first in the page's opening
sentence. The belief sentence after it keeps Fatema's words, with its list
changed from "strategy, content, and editorial thinking" (the founders' three
crafts only) to "strategy, content and craft".
